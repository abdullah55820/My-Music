Dial 1.8.2

### Player
- When you skip from a Canvas, the Canvas fades into the cover of the subsequent song. The cover of the last song does not show again.
- A music video that is used as a Canvas also fades into the subsequent cover. When you skip back to it, it comes back immediately without the cover first.
- A music video that has a song version shows the cover of the song from the start. It does not show the video thumbnail first.
- The cover in the mini player fades into the subsequent cover when you skip.
- The queue does not bounce at the end of a swipe-down close or a back swipe.
- When the queue is open, a swipe down on the playing song closes the queue. It does not close the player.
- With the compact Canvas controls, a song with no lyrics shows its title and artist directly above the controls.
- With "Hide status bar" on, a swipe down from the top edge shows the status bar. It does not make the player small. A second swipe opens the notifications.

### Menus
- A back swipe on a menu that slides up makes the menu smaller. Then the menu closes. If you stop the swipe, the menu becomes full size again.
- The lyrics offset menu, the Automix mix view, the language windows, the lyrics sources window and the "Show all" moods sheet use the app style (Glass, Frost or Solid).

### Home and navigation
- In the Frost style, the category chips on the dial page have the same colour as the chips under the top bar.
- In the Frost and Solid styles, the selection pill of the navigation bar stays fully in the bar. It is not cut.
- A tap on a tab of the navigation bar does not show a second, smaller shape in the pill.

### Discord
- A crash is corrected. The app ran out of memory some seconds after you turned on Discord rich presence.
- A song with no web cover, for example a local file, shows a Dial picture on your Discord status.
- A local file does not show the listen button on your Discord status.
- The profile card on the Discord settings page uses the app style.
- Note: Discord does not show the buttons of your status to you. Other people see them.

### Settings
- Performance, Video quality: the mobile data line has a new option, Off. With Off, on mobile data, the app does not load a Canvas or a video. Music videos and podcast episodes play as audio with their cover.
- Sign out and Disconnect rows are red (YouTube Music, Spotify, Discord, WebDAV and SMB).
